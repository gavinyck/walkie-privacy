Privacy Policy

App: Walkie Treadmill (走走)  |  Last updated: October 18, 2026

GGG Studio ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Walkie Treadmill application for Android (the "App"). This page explains what the App does and does not do with your information.

In short: Walkie Treadmill does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The App does not request internet access. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDK of any kind.

Information We Collect

None. We operate no servers and receive no data about you.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored locally on your device and is never sent to us or any third party:

  • Workout history — date, duration, distance, estimated calories and elevation gain of each workout
  • Course progress and achievements — multi-day expedition progress, unlocked achievements and your best times
  • Body weight — entered by you, used only to estimate calories
  • Settings — such as automatic speed, speed limits, weekly goal, vibration and the last random course

Uninstalling the App deletes all of it. If Android device backup is turned on, the system may include this data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your own account; we have no access to it.

Treadmill Connection

The App connects to your treadmill over Bluetooth Low Energy using the standard Fitness Machine Service (FTMS). It reads speed, incline and elapsed time from the treadmill, and sends commands to start, stop and change speed or incline. This communication happens directly between your device and the treadmill.

Voice Prompts

Voice prompts are spoken by the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The App only sends it short, fixed phrases such as the next incline or speed. The engine is provided by your device or a third party and is subject to its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth scan & connect) — to find and connect to your treadmill. On Android 12 and later, the scan is declared as not used to derive location.
  • Location (Android 11 and earlier only) — required by those Android versions to scan for Bluetooth devices. The App never reads your location.
  • Vibration — for the optional vibration alert before the speed increases.

Health and Fitness Information

Distance, calories and elevation are estimates calculated from the speed and incline reported by the treadmill. They are for reference only and are not intended for medical use or diagnosis.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, if advertising is added in a future version — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised.
